Document Overview
This PRD defines a proposed unified digital platform for Simhastha 2028 in Ujjain. The platform is intended to combine pilgrimage planning, spiritual and cultural information, GIS navigation, live crowd and traffic information, emergency services, lost-and-found, notifications, AI assistance, and an administrative command platform.
1. Product Vision
One Digital Platform for Every Pilgrim, Every Service, Every Journey.
The platform should provide a trusted digital destination for pilgrims while also acting as a real-time operational and information layer for participating government departments.
2. Problem Statement
Simhastha creates a high-density, time-sensitive environment where millions of visitors may need navigation, accommodation, transportation, spiritual information, medical support, emergency assistance and reliable public announcements. The platform must reduce information fragmentation and help people make safe, informed decisions.
3. Product Goals
Provide a comprehensive digital pilgrimage guide.
Provide real-time crowd, traffic, parking and route information.
Improve access to emergency and safety services.
Provide authoritative spiritual, cultural and event information.
Support multilingual and accessibility-friendly experiences.
Provide administrators with GIS-based operational visibility.
Enable AI-assisted search and question answering grounded in authoritative data.
Support offline access through a Progressive Web App (PWA).
Create a single authoritative data layer for public and administrative channels.

46. Future Digital Twin
A future Ujjain Simhastha Digital Twin can combine GIS, crowd, traffic, parking, infrastructure and operational data to simulate scenarios such as increased crowd density in a specific zone. Simulation outputs should support, not replace, authorized human operational decisions.
47. Success Criteria
A pilgrim can discover Simhastha information without visiting multiple unrelated portals.
A pilgrim can plan a trip and navigate to verified destinations.
Users can find emergency and essential services quickly.
The public platform can publish verified real-time operational information.
Administrators can view operational status on a unified GIS dashboard.
Critical incidents have defined escalation and audit workflows.
The platform remains usable under severe traffic spikes.
AI answers are grounded in authoritative sources and current APIs.
Privacy and security controls are auditable.
48. Key Product Principle
The platform should not be treated as a conventional tourism website. It should be designed as Digital Public Infrastructure + Pilgrimage Platform + GIS System + Emergency/Safety Platform + Real-Time Operations Platform + AI Assistant.
A single authoritative Simhastha data layer/API should be established from the beginning so that the website, PWA, mobile application, command center and AI assistant consume consistent information. This reduces the risk of conflicting public information across channels.
